Week 3 · Planning Our Dataset

What we did in the workshop

Our group chose Option A and decided to look at how international students use generative AI when they run into language difficulties in their studies.

Our research question

How does generative AI help international students overcome language barriers in their studies?

We wanted to look for examples of students using AI to understand course readings, translate unfamiliar terms or improve their English writing. We planned to use public social media posts and comments as secondary data. My part was to collect examples from TikTok and Douyin.

For each video, we planned to record the platform, the number of likes, the tool recommended, the likes on a relevant comment and the country where the creator said they were studying, if they mentioned it.

My reflection

In Week 2, I knew what I wanted to collect straight away: BBC headlines. This time, I had to think more about what should go into the spreadsheet. A video might recommend a translation app without giving an example of anyone using it for their studies. I wanted to look for those examples because they could tell me more about what students actually used the tool for.

I also wondered what the likes would tell us. If one video gets more likes than another, it might simply have reached more people. The comments could give us more detail, especially if someone explains how they used a tool to get through a difficult reading. Even then, we'd only be hearing from people who chose to post or comment. I'd keep that in mind when comparing what I find on TikTok and Douyin.
